# Competitors & Examples

## Commercial competitors (and what they prove about the market)

| App | What they do well | Where they fall short | Money / traction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yousician** | Real-time pitch detection while you play; gamified songs; guitar/piano/bass/uke/voice | Reviewers: "lacks … rather crucial blackboard information, namely theory and technical explanations" | 20M+ monthly active users across its products (incl. GuitarTuna); raised $28M Series B |
| **Simply Guitar / Simply Piano** (JoyTunes → "Simply") | Extremely polished onboarding; mic-based feedback | No instruction at all for slides, bends, hammer-ons, or pull-offs — songs, not technique | $1B valuation (2021), ~$100M revenue in 2020, $93M+ raised — proof this market pays |
| **JustinGuitar** | Best-in-class graded lesson structure; beloved free content | No listening/feedback loop; video-only | Sustained for years on donations + paid app; huge goodwill moat |
| **StringKick** | Teaches theory/"the language" well | No structured progression or real-time feedback | Content/courses business |
| **Duolingo** (model, not competitor) | The unlocking skill-map progression we're borrowing; proves streaks + short lessons retain beginners | Not music | Public company — the retention model demonstrably pays |
| **Rocksmith+ (Ubisoft)** | Real-audio note detection at scale | Song-first, subscription fatigue, no theory spine | Major-publisher backing shows demand for "listens while you play" |
| **Soundslice** | Best-in-class web notation/tab player synced to audio/video; practice looping | A practice *tool*, not a curriculum | Profitable indie SaaS; licenses its player — "stuff that pays well" without VC |
| **Ultimate Guitar / Songsterr** | Tabs are how guitarists actually read — massive libraries | No teaching, no feedback | Long-running subscription businesses on tabs alone |

**The gap Harmonic targets:** nobody combines StringKick-style theory,
JustinGuitar-style progression, and Yousician-style listening with feedback that's
better than binary correct/incorrect.

## What users actually complain about, per app

Researched Sept 2026 from app-store reviews, Trustpilot, ComplaintsBoard, Metacritic,
Steam, and community forums. Verbatim quotes live in
[user-pain-stories.md](user-pain-stories.md).

### Yousician
- **No theory / "blackboard" explanations** — lessons "touch a topic then move on
  never to revisit it," leaving "huge holes." ([Guitar Chalk](https://www.guitarchalk.com/yousician-review/))
- **Binary right/wrong feedback, no technique detection** — "no way to tell if you
  are developing bad habits"; called "a metronome with ears" that ignores vibrato,
  slurs, groove, expression. ([Producer Hive](https://producerhive.com/buyer-guides/learn/yousician-review/), [TheGuitarLesson](https://www.theguitarlesson.com/guitar-lesson-blog/guitar-lesson-review/yousician-review/))
- **Note detection marks correct playing wrong** — fails to register open strings
  (acoustic G string); beginners doubt themselves. ([ComplaintsBoard](https://www.complaintsboard.com/yousician-b149861))
- **Shallow exercise-like content** — "The songs you play don't sound good. They are
  like exercises, not songs."
- **Gamification novelty wears off**; loop prioritizes stars over understanding.
- **Aggressive paywall + billing surprises** — content locks after ~3 lessons;
  surprise $180 annual charges from a "free 7 day trial."
- **Difficulty spikes with no tempo control** — songs jump "4 levels above where I
  was last time"; the app "doesn't even let you choose the tempo."
- **Song library churn** — favorited songs vanish; an update wiped "90% of their
  artist and song catalogue." ([App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yousician-learn-play-guitar/id959883039?see-all=reviews))

### Simply Guitar & Simply Piano
- **Mic detection fails on open/low strings, lags, and false-passes** — a
  Guitar.com tester's *cough* registered as a perfect Fmaj7 and a muted scrape as
  E minor; Simply Piano marks deliberately wrong chords correct. ([Guitar.com](https://guitar.com/reviews/accessories/simply-guitar-review/))
- **Hard-stuck on broken detection → quitting** — progression is gated on lessons
  the mic can't hear: users stuck on one level for weeks, sent back to the start on
  any miss, kids and adults giving up entirely. ([JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1476695335/simply-guitar-by-joytunes/reviews))
- **Binary feedback with no diagnosis** — no way to distinguish wrong note from
  wrong timing; the only failure response is looping into practice mode.
- **Paywall after ~one free lesson** (~$60/3 months) — "When it says its free in the
  app store or play store, that is a dead lie." ([ComplaintsBoard](https://www.complaintsboard.com/simply-guitar-learn-guitar-b149707))
- **No technique instruction, stalls at early-intermediate** — "complete absence of
  instruction for slides, bends, hammer-ons, and pull-offs"; no metronome.
- **Forced linear pacing** — can't skip sections or set a practice tempo. ([Piano Dreamers](https://www.pianodreamers.com/simply-piano-review/))
- **Broken detection trains bad habits** — one beginner strummed harder and harder
  to force registration until "this caused blisters."

### Rocksmith / Rocksmith+
- **Unreliable note detection without the Real Tone cable** — clean hits register as
  misses, especially on amplified electric. ([NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/rocksmith-review-bloated-but-undeniably-useful-3304271))
- **Wrong grading locks difficulty** — 50–60% accuracy scores on trivial beginner
  parts keep dynamic difficulty pinned low. ([Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/rocksmith-plus/user-reviews/))
- **Subscription backlash + delisted songs** — ~$100+/yr replaced a one-time
  purchase; ~50 songs removed while subscribers were paying. ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2834910/discussions/0/597389252535308982/?ctp=2))
- **The note highway teaches the game, not music reading** — "Game is UNREADABLE.
  Repeatable chords are impossible to sight read."
- **Detection failures get blamed on the learner** — the community answers
  missed-note complaints with "you pressed too hard," so beginners can't tell if
  they played wrong or the app heard wrong.

### Video lessons (JustinGuitar, YouTube, Fender Play)
- **No feedback loop at all** — a learner quit guitar because he "didn't receive any
  good feedback which really discouraged me"; bad habits get discovered years later. ([JustinGuitar community](https://community.justinguitar.com/t/abhis-log-what-guitar-taught-me-a-journey-of-failure-persistence-and-happiness/413914))
- **Undiagnosable plateaus** — stuck on one solo for 10 months: "it's difficult to
  figure out what is blocking me." ([JustinGuitar community](https://community.justinguitar.com/t/hit-a-plateau/403010))
- **Post-beginner direction vacuum** — 2 years in, solid theory, and still: "I feel
  stuck as in i don't know where to go next." ([JustinGuitar community](https://community.justinguitar.com/t/dont-know-what-to-practice/242745))
- **Fixed video pacing, can't ask questions** — "I kinda need someone showing me
  what to play instead of Justin saying, just try it it's the blues!"
- **Motivation decay and quit-restart cycles**; **tutorial hell** — watching videos
  feels like progress while skills stagnate.
- **Fender Play is shallow** — one user "burned through the whole thing in a week." ([Deviant Noise](https://deviantnoise.net/education/guitar/fender-play-review/))

### Tab & notation sites (Ultimate Guitar, Songsterr, MuseScore)
- **Cancellation dark patterns (UG)** — trials converting to ~$99 annual charges;
  cancellations that "don't stick" year after year. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ultimate-guitar.com))
- **MuseScore "buy one score" bait-and-switch** — a $0.99–$6.49 purchase enrolls you
  in a $44.99–$69.99 recurring subscription (one user: $324.95); at least one bank
  hard-blocked MuseScore payments over chargebacks. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/musescore.com), [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45384632))
- **Crowd-sourced tabs are frequently wrong and unverified** — "none of the websites
  actually test them out first"; whole songs transcribed on one string. ([AnandTech forums](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/why-are-guitar-tabs-so-damn-wrong-all-the-time.8490/))
- **Plain tabs omit rhythm/duration** — beginners can't tell how a song should sound
  unless they already know it (Songsterr's entire pitch exists because of this gap).
- **Zero feedback loop** — static references with no listening or assessment.

### Voice / singing apps (Yousician Singing, Vanido, Sing Sharp, Voice Training)
- **Self-diagnosed "tone deafness" causes pre-emptive quitting** — adults write
  themselves off even though true amusia is rare and pitch-matching is trainable.
- **Perception vs. production gap, undiagnosed** — many users can *hear* notes
  accurately but can't produce them; no app tells them which problem they have. ([Frontiers in Psychology](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00164/full))
- **Sight-singing gaps cause real social embarrassment** — coming in a beat early
  alone in choir was "mortifying"; a trained pianist was rejected from a university
  choir for not knowing vocal sight reading.
- **Broken pitch detection destroys trust** — one app marked a note correct *before
  the user sang*; Vanido shipped a bug tracking every note a half-step flat;
  Yousician singing fails with any background noise.
- **Grades pitch but teaches no technique** — "only feedback about hitting
  individual notes," never chest vs. head voice. ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1130249200/vanido-learn-to-sing/reviews))
- **No audiation scaffolding** — apps show the piano key but won't *play* the
  reference pitch; beginners can't sing a note by name without hearing it first.

### Gamification model (Duolingo and its imitators)
- **Streak compulsion divorced from learning** — users continue years after wanting
  to quit; **a broken streak is an abandonment cliff** (accounts go dead immediately
  after a lost streak). ([arXiv study](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.16175))
- **Gaming XP instead of learning** — XP farming and leaderboard grinding destroyed
  users' drive and confidence.
- **Shallow learning that doesn't transfer** — multi-year streaks, still can't hold
  a basic conversation; "users chase streaks, not skills."
- **What demonstrably works: the habit loop** — ~3× session time vs. Babbel, 110M+
  actives. Lesson for Harmonic: keep the daily loop, make rewards track real skill,
  and make missed days recoverable rather than catastrophic.

## Open-source projects to learn from (or borrow)

| Project | What it is | Why it matters to us |
|---|---|---|
| [PickHero](https://github.com/Artemarius/PickHero) | Free, open-source desktop guitar practice app: real-time pitch detection (YIN algorithm), scrolling tab playback, reads Guitar Pro files — a self-described "lightweight Yousician alternative" | The closest OSS analog to our guitar feature; study its pitch-detection + tab-sync approach |
| [SightreadingPractice](https://github.com/DavidCEllis/SightreadingPractice) | Generates random sheet music, reads MIDI/audio input to check notes; VexFlow display, experimented with the Crepe (TensorFlow) pitch tracker | Directly prefigures our "generated random melody" sight-reading feature |
| [MuseScore](https://github.com/musescore/MuseScore) | The open-source notation editor; huge score ecosystem | Our import target format; its file handling is reference code |
| [alphaTab](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab) | Open-source notation + guitar tab rendering with built-in synth playback | Already our core rendering pick — it's OSS, so we can read the source when stuck |
| [VexFlow](https://github.com/0xfe/vexflow) | Open-source standard-notation renderer | Fallback renderer; used by SightreadingPractice above |
| [OpenSheetMusicDisplay](https://github.com/opensheetmusicdisplay/opensheetmusicdisplay) | Open-source MusicXML renderer built on VexFlow | If MusicXML import becomes central, this is the proven path |
| [TuxGuitar](https://github.com/helge17/tuxguitar) | Long-lived open-source Guitar Pro-style tab editor/player | Reference for tab playback semantics |
| GitHub topics: [music-education](https://github.com/topics/music-education), [pitch-detection](https://github.com/topics/pitch-detection), [sight-reading](https://github.com/topics/sight-reading), [ear-training](https://github.com/topics/ear-training), [chord-detection](https://github.com/topics/chord-detection) | Curated ecosystems | Ongoing mining ground for prior art (e.g., Bemol, an OSS relative-pitch ear trainer) |

## Takeaways

1. **The market pays.** JoyTunes hit unicorn status and ~$100M revenue on *songs
   without theory*; Yousician holds 20M MAU with *binary feedback*. A better product
   thesis has room. And per Fender's CEO, **90% of beginners quit within a year** —
   the industry monetizes the surviving 10% ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/90-of-beginner-guitar-players-give-up-within-a-year-says-fender)).
2. **The deeper pain isn't just binary feedback — it's *untrustworthy* feedback.**
   Every detection-based app shows the same compound failure: pass/fail with no
   diagnosis, AND a pass/fail signal that's frequently wrong (false misses on open
   strings, false passes on wrong chords, a cough scored as Fmaj7). Users can't tell
   whether they played wrong or the app heard wrong. Harmonic's feedback must be
   diagnostic *and* demonstrably reliable, or it inherits this trust deficit.
3. **Detection bugs are a direct burnout accelerant.** Because progression is gated
   on broken detection, users get hard-stuck through no fault of their playing — a
   10-year-old throwing his guitar in the corner, an adult "hesitating on touching
   my guitar again." The app's bug becomes the learner's self-doubt. Our guided
   mode's failsafes (3-miss skip, hints, manual override) exist precisely to prevent
   this.
4. **New pain we hadn't listed: the post-beginner direction vacuum.** Independent
   reports across JustinGuitar, Fender Play, and Simply describe learners who finish
   beginner content and simply don't know what to practice next. No competitor
   addresses it — an opening for Harmonic's progression to extend past the beginner
   cliff.
5. **Billing dark patterns are the loudest public anger in the category.**
   Trial-to-$99 conversions and buy-one-score subscription traps dominate 1-star
   reviews — transparent pricing is a cheap differentiator.
6. **The hard parts have prior art.** Real-time pitch detection (PickHero, Crepe,
   YIN/McLeod), notation rendering (alphaTab, VexFlow, OSMD), and random-melody
   generation (SightreadingPractice) all exist in the open — we integrate and
   differentiate, we don't invent from scratch.
7. **Indie-profitable is a real path.** Soundslice and JustinGuitar show this niche
   sustains businesses without unicorn scale — relevant to our TestFlight/PWA
   distribution question.
