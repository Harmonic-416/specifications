# User Stories

## Beginner guitarist

- As a beginner trying to learn guitar from an app, I've tried what's on the market
  and hated it — the apps grade me pass/fail without teaching me why anything works.
  I want short lessons that explain the concept first, so I understand the language
  instead of memorizing songs.
- As a beginner guitarist, I want the app to listen while I play and tell me
  *specifically* what went wrong (muted string, fuzzy chord from not pressing hard
  enough, late strum, wrong chord), so I can fix the actual problem instead of
  guessing.
- As a beginner guitarist, I want lessons on an unlocking map that rises in
  difficulty gradually, so I don't jump into complexity that makes me quit.
- As a guitarist, I want to read **tabs** (with standard notation alongside), because
  that's what guitarists actually use.

## Singer / sight-reader

- As an acapella singer, I want sight-reading practice with pitch detection where the
  music doesn't advance until I sing the right note, so I build accuracy note by note.
- As a singer practicing in guided mode, I want failsafes — after 3 misses the note
  is skipped and recorded in my score, I can skip manually, and I can ask for a
  "hint" that plays the note once — so I never get permanently stuck.
- As a more confident singer, I want a **practice mode** that plays straight through
  without stopping, then shows me what I sang vs. the score, so I can rehearse
  performance conditions.
- As a singer who uses solfège, I want to toggle solfège labels on; as one who
  doesn't, I want them off.
- As a singer, I want to sight-read **randomly generated melodies**, so I can't
  memorize my practice material.

## Practicing user (any instrument)

- As a user, I want a playlist of practice pieces where each song shows its sheet
  music, plays reference audio (toggleable), and lets me click or drag to any spot to
  start playback there.
- As a user, I want a summary of my practice run-throughs — last score, a recording
  of my attempt, and highlighted patchy/spotty areas on the music — so I know exactly
  what to drill next.
- As a user, I want to import my own MusicXML/MuseScore/MIDI files, so I can practice
  the music I actually care about.

## Future / stretch stories

- As an acapella group, we want group sessions where multiple people/instruments join
  one song and collaborators can leave notes on practiced pieces.
- As a guitarist, I want camera-based feedback on finger placement relative to the
  frets (computer vision stretch goal).
- As a user, I want to play along to a YouTube video and see how well I matched it.
